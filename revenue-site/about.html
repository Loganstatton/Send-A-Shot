About QuickTools

QuickTools is a small collection of free, fast utilities — a JSON formatter, word counter, password generator, QR code generator, and more — that run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or paste is uploaded to a server; the pages are static and the tools are plain JavaScript.

The goal is simple: solve the specific small task you searched for, quickly, without an account, a paywall, or a cluttered page.
