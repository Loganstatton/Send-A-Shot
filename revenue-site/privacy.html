What this site does with your input

Every tool on QuickTools runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Text, JSON, passwords, or any other content you type or paste into a tool on this site is never sent to a server, logged, or stored by us. Closing or reloading the page discards it.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show advertisements served by third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or your ad network's own opt-out page. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet — see Google's advertising policy for details.

Analytics

This site may use a privacy-respecting analytics service to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. page views, referrers) without tracking individuals across other sites. No personal data is sold.
