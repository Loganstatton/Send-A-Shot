# Lead Rescue — "The Missed Call" | Cinematic 3D Ad Script

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (1080×1920) · **Runtime:** 19s · **Style:** Dark premium SaaS/tech, realistic 3D smartphone (no third-party branding), cinematic camera, no dialogue required — fully readable muted.

A synced interactive preview of this timeline lives at `public/ads/lead-rescue-cinematic-ad.html` (open it in a browser, or see `PREVIEW.md` for how to capture it as video).

---

## Visual language

- **Environment:** infinite dark studio void — near-black gradient (`#05060a` → `#0c0f18`), soft volumetric haze, a single cool rim light (electric blue, `#3b82f6`/`#22d3ee`) and one warm key light (amber, `#f59e0b`) used only for the "value" and "recovered" beats.
- **Phone:** realistic unbranded slab — matte aluminum frame, glass screen, centered pill-shaped camera cutout (no logo, no wordmark on the chassis). Floats mid-frame on a slow idle bob/rotate; shadow pooled beneath it on an unseen glass floor for grounding.
- **Typography:** one clean geometric sans, weight varies for hierarchy, never more than 2 lines on screen at once, minimum 1.5s hold on any headline.
- **Lead Rescue mark:** a rounded-square badge containing a bolt-through-shield glyph, paired with the wordmark "Lead Rescue" in the same sans, electric-blue accent.

## Sound design (optional — ad must work muted)

- 0–5s: soft phone ring (real ringtone feel, not cartoonish), rising low synth pad under the "value" beat.
- 6.5s: hard low thud / riser cut on the MISSED CALL flash.
- 9.5s: rising digital "activation" whoosh + soft chime on the Lead Rescue wave.
- 11–14s: two quiet message "pop" blips (outbound, then inbound).
- 14.5s: satisfying resolve chime on LEAD RECOVERED.
- 17s+: music swells into a confident, minimal closing sting under the CTA.

## Delivery notes

- Keep every text beat isolated — never more than one headline + one caption on screen together, per the brief's "don't overcrowd" direction.
- The story must read correctly at 2x speed skim (common for Reels/TikTok scroll-past): ringing phone → red missed call → avatar leaving → blue rescue pulse → text sent/received → green recovered → logo/CTA.
- Loop point: the final card can hard-cut back to the 0.0s cold open for endless autoplay loops in feed placements.
